# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and useful. Disagreement is fine. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, and deliberate exposure of private account data are not.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- giving reproducible bug reports
- reviewing code and translations in good faith
- correcting mistakes without attacking the person who made them
- protecting private data in screenshots, logs, and fixtures
- accepting a maintainer's decision to preserve the project's narrow security model

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- publishing another person's bookmark export or account data
- sharing tokens, cookies, passwords, or private browser data
- harassment, threats, or discriminatory language
- spam, scams, or unrelated promotion
- attempting to pressure contributors into weakening privacy protections without technical review

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems through the repository's private security-reporting channel when the report contains sensitive information. Public moderation issues may be raised with the repository owner through GitHub.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and other project spaces.
